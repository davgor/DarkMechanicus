# Mascot action sprite atlases

Seven transparent raster atlases for the chibi tech priest mascot are in `src/renderer/src/assets/mascot/`. Playback contract and explicit row-major frame rectangles are in [`atlas.json`](../src/renderer/src/assets/mascot/atlas.json). Every sheet is 1536×1024 px: three columns by two rows of 512×512 px cells. Frames share the same cell origin; transparent padding is part of each cell. Each frame has its own local `footAnchor` measured from the alpha-visible contact extent to avoid baseline jumps. The action-level `footAnchor` is the fallback. Apply each action `renderScale` to the nominal cell display size and to both coordinates of the source-local frame anchor; frame rectangles remain unchanged. `jump` and `climb` use `renderScale: 0.78` to normalize their larger generated character drawings against the other four actions (`1.0`). For jump playback, the runtime supplies vertical trajectory movement while pinning the scaled frame-local anchor. Walk and run face right and may be mirrored for left-facing playback. Idle, walk, run, and climb loop; jump and stumble play once.

`drill.png` adds a separate six-frame loop selected by the optional `working` flag on `MascotSprite`; it does not add an action to `MotionAction`. The character stays foot-anchored while the small drill moves between raised, contact, recoil, and ready poses. `resting` freezes whichever action is selected at `motion.elapsed`, so paused drill playback does not advance its sparks or poses. The preview page includes the live loop and a fixed drill frame. Opaque subject bounds (alpha >128), in row-major source-local coordinates, are `(129,86)-(408,443)`, `(133,93)-(391,447)`, `(121,91)-(387,445)`, `(123,51)-(435,413)`, `(125,68)-(399,415)`, and `(103,51)-(399,412)`. At alpha >16, bounds are `(128,85)-(409,444)`, `(132,92)-(392,447)`, `(121,90)-(388,448)`, `(122,51)-(436,413)`, `(124,67)-(399,418)`, and `(103,50)-(399,412)`. Raw alpha >0 extends broadly on a few cells, to as far as `(1,88)-(422,508)` and `(7,23)-(474,440)`, due to low-opacity generated color haze outside the character. ImageGen framing/removal edits did not eliminate it; the earlier walk atlas has similarly broad nonzero-alpha extents. No raster cleanup was applied.

## Validation

For a local visual check, run `npx vite --config preview/mascot/vite.config.ts --host 127.0.0.1 --port 5178` from the repository root. Open `http://127.0.0.1:5178/preview/mascot/actions.html` for the six motion atlases and the seventh drill atlas. Open `http://127.0.0.1:5178/preview/mascot/graph.html` for the real PlanGraph preview. Four linked tickets form a climbable stack, with the top and bottom tickets running. The mascot uses intermediate cards to reach the highest running ticket, pauses to grip, sometimes slips, and drills with contact sparks when supported. Complete the upper task to see him retarget the lower one. A rapid left-right-left user pan can knock him down once; a lower card may catch him, and a fall from the lowest card lands on the floor. Gentle pans, zoom and Fit View do not count as shakes. Ticket selection, drag/drop, pan, zoom and resize remain available. Pause persists; hidden pages and reduced motion stop the animation. These development-only pages are separate Vite pages, not app navigation routes or production entries.

The original six saved PNGs were inspected in a browser on neutral backgrounds. Each is 1536×1024, RGBA, with six recognizable action poses. Sharp metadata and per-cell alpha-bounds inspection verified that each silhouette stays inside its own 512×512 rect after the framing edits. Transparent background samples have alpha 0 and mascot samples have near-opaque alpha; the red-looking preview in some image viewers is their transparency composite, not painted background. Frame rectangles and both per-frame and fallback action foot anchors are explicit in `atlas.json`. Per-frame local y anchors were measured from the bottommost alpha-visible pixel at alpha >128; x is cell center (256). This keeps grounded contacts aligned across changes in pose.
