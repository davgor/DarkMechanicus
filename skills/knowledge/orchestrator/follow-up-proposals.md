id: orchestrator.follow-up-proposals
type: convention
title: "Follow-up ideas are proposals, not tickets you add"
scope.roles: [orchestrator]
scope.events: [ticket.claimed]
scope.topics: [plan-changes, retro-redraft]
priority: 60
---

Follow-up ideas from workers or reports go to the person or the planner as proposals. Do not add tickets to a running plan yourself, except in the redraft loop (`get_context` topic `retro-redraft`), which is the one exception and adds tickets only to future sprints.
