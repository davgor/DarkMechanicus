title: "Stop sooner on fruitless diagnostics; a stop is no unsafe termination"

- Stop expanding even sooner after two diagnostic rounds that close no named acceptance gap. Each round needs a hypothesis, discriminating observation and exit condition. Reassess the bounded next experiment or design; do not churn native permissions, fixtures or probes to keep a lease alive. An inconclusive feasibility gate blocks dependent implementation.
- A stop is not authority for unsafe abrupt native termination. Preserve original finite invocation/cleanup deadlines, ownership and consent boundaries; finish only the already-authorized safe containment/cleanup protocol and record blocked or uncertain outcomes honestly. Never claim cleanup or success without evidence.
- Only an unavoidable noninterruptible automated verification declared in the saved plan BEFORE claim may exceed 10 minutes. Enforce its exact scope and finite deadline, without new implementation or retroactive timeout growth. Keep the full Definition of Done at actual sprint acceptance and targeted checks per ticket; no weaker acceptance, new approval gates or per-ticket full suites.
